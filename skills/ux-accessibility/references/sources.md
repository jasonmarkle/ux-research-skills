# Sources

Full references for `SKILL.md`. Standards are referred to by success criterion number and name; the wording in the skill is original and is not a substitute for the standard itself.

## Standards and W3C guidance

- W3C. *Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2*. W3C Recommendation, first published 5 October 2023; current edition 12 December 2024. https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
- W3C. *Understanding WCAG 2.2*. https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/
- W3C. *ARIA Authoring Practices Guide (APG)*. https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/
- W3C. *Using ARIA*. W3C draft, retired 24 February 2026. Its first rule, to prefer a native HTML element where one exists, remains standard advice and is repeated in the ARIA Authoring Practices Guide. https://www.w3.org/TR/using-aria/
- W3C. (2021). *Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities*. W3C Working Group Note, 29 April 2021. https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/
- W3C. (2019). *Inaccessibility of CAPTCHA: Alternatives to Visual Turing Tests on the Web*. W3C Working Group Note, 9 December 2019 (a draft update was published 16 December 2021). https://www.w3.org/TR/turingtest/
